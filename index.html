For parents · பெற்றோருக்கு

Vazhi lists only government, aided and merit-based institutions. No college pays to appear here, and we have nothing to sell — what your child sees is chosen on merit alone.

  • 📅 Check upcoming exam & application dates — the deadlines that decide admissions.
  • 📚 Browse courses & colleges — filter by your child's Class 12 subjects to see every realistic option.
  • 💰 See scholarships — fee waivers mean a low-income family can study at an IIT almost free.
  • 🧠 Have your child take the Know Yourself test — 12 minutes, available in Tamil, gives a printable career report you can discuss together.

A government or aided college seat usually costs a fraction of a private one — often with stronger faculty, alumni and placements. Your child has more good options than you may have heard.

For teachers & counsellors

Everything on Vazhi is free to use in your classroom or counselling sessions.

  • 🔍 Career guide — type any career a student names and get the Class 12 subjects → exam → college path for it.
  • 📅 Key dates — ready-made content for your notice board announcements.
  • 🧠 Know Yourself test — a Holland RIASEC interest test students can take on any phone, in English or Tamil.
  • 📖 Glossary — 40 admission terms (NIRF, LPA, lateral entry…) explained in English and Tamil, via the "Key Terms" button.

Suggestions or corrections from educators are very welcome — write to kalvivazhi@gmail.com.

Which course should I choose?

No course guarantees a salary — your skills and consistency decide that. Some careers pay from day one, others grow with experience. What courses really differ in is how proven, how new, or how uncrowded they are.
