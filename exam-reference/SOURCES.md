## Annual refresh procedure

1. Re-check every ✅ row — they date to July 2026 and go stale within a year.
2. Resolve ❓ rows from official sites when preparing the Vazhi version.
3. Re-derive interpretive bands (score/rank ladders) from the latest MCC / JoSAA / consortium cutoff data.
4. Registration figures: always distinguish **registered vs appeared vs unique-across-sessions**.
5. Seat figures: always distinguish **sanctioned vs filled**, and **total vs govt-only**.
