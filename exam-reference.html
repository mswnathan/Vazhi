Flagship Exam — NEET UG

One exam decides admission to every medical, dental, and AYUSH UG seat in India — govt and private. There is no other route into MBBS. Every PCB/PCMB student should register for it, regardless of final career direction, because it also opens BDS, BAMS, BHMS, BSMS, BUMS, BNYS, B.Sc Nursing and BVSc.

Flagship Exams — JEE Main & JEE Advanced

JEE Main alone gets you into 31 NITs, 26 IIITs, and GFTIs. Qualifying in the top 2.5 lakh opens JEE Advanced, the gateway to all 23 IITs. Every PCM/PCMB student aiming for a national engineering seat should write JEE Main — most state CETs are a fallback, not a replacement.

Flagship Exam — CUET-UG

Replaced individual entrance tests for Delhi University, JNU, BHU, Hyderabad Central, Pondicherry University, AMU and 40+ other central universities. It's the main route into a central university for any stream — Science, Commerce or Arts.

❓
Do You Need CUET?
Take CUET if... You want a UG seat at a Central University — Delhi University, JNU, BHU, Hyderabad Central, or Pondicherry University (the nearest one for TN & Puducherry students). Commerce and Arts students especially — CUET is the main route to top central universities for B.Com, BA Economics and similar degrees.
You do NOT need CUET if... You're applying to Tamil Nadu government colleges — those admit on Class 12 marks (Arts & Science) or TNEA rank (Engineering).

Indian Institutes of Science Education and Research — 7 premier institutes, prestige equivalent to the IITs but focused purely on science.
