About Vazhi

ஒவ்வொரு கனவுக்கும் சரியான வழி தேவை

Vazhi (வழி — “the path”) is short for KalviVazhi — கல்வி வழி, “the education path” — which is also our address: kalvivazhi.in.

Vazhi is built by a software engineer with 25+ years in the IT industry — not by a coaching institute or an ed-tech company.

It began with a simple observation: when students ask for guidance, even teaching professionals rarely mention anything beyond JEE and NEET. Two exams, out of the hundreds of courses and government institutions India actually offers. Students weren't choosing from few options because the options don't exist — but because no one had shown them.

Vazhi exists to close that gap: every course, every government and merit-based institution, and the entrance exam for each — in one place, so students and parents can make informed decisions instead of crowd-following ones.

What Vazhi covers

  • 70+ UG courses — from MBBS and B.Tech to actuarial science, audiology, and film — each with its entrance exam and career paths
  • 1,800+ colleges across Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, and every major Indian state
  • Entrance exams for UG and PG, with syllabus topics and participating institutes
  • After-UG pathways — higher education, research, government jobs, study abroad
  • Scholarships and internships — government, PSU, and merit-based
  • Know Yourself — a free interest test (in English and Tamil) with a printable career report
  • Rank predictors for JoSAA (IIT/NIT/IIIT) and TNEA counselling

What Vazhi will not do

Vazhi lists only government, government-aided, and merit-based institutions. Private self-financing colleges are not listed — not because they are all bad, but because every other website already promotes them, and none of them needed help being found.

Where the information comes from

Course, exam, and college details are compiled from official sources — exam-conducting bodies (NTA, ICAR, NCHMCT and others), institution websites, NIRF rankings, and government admission portals — and refreshed as new notifications appear.

Always verify dates and eligibility on the official website before applying. Vazhi is a guidance aid, not an official notification service.
