What is IISER?

Indian Institutes of Science Education and Research — 7 premier institutes set up by MHRD. Equivalent in prestige to IITs but focused purely on science. The BS-MS here is a fully funded 5-year integrated degree — low fees, and students receive a monthly fellowship from Day 1. It is the best route in India for a student who loves Biology as a science, not just as a NEET subject.

3 Routes into IISER
1. IAT — IISER Aptitude Test The direct exam. Write in June after Class 12. Open to all students.
2. JEE Advanced Channel Strong JEE Advanced rank → direct IISER admission (no IAT needed).
3. SCB — State / Central Board Channel Top rank in state board (within a cut-off percentile) → eligible without IAT.

When to Recommend IISER IAT to This Student
✅ Recommend if she says any of these "I love Biology as a subject, not just for NEET"  ·  "I'm curious about how things work"  ·  "I like lab work"  ·  "I want to go abroad for higher studies"  ·  "I'm okay with a non-clinical career"
⚠️ Mention but don't push if Primary goal is treating patients  ·  Parents firmly want "Doctor" title  ·  Math is weak (extra prep needed for Math section)
❌ Don't recommend as primary if She's scoring 550+ consistently in NEET mocks — all energy should go to NEET. IISER is for the student who needs a route that honours her biology preparation without requiring NEET success.
The core strategy One year of NEET preparation covers most of these exams. Register early for all relevant ones. Write them all. Results will show the options — then she decides.
